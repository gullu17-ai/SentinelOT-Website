# SentinelOT: Product Studio redesign

Direction approved by the user on 15 November 2026. Anti Slop skills are not used for this redesign.

## Audience and identity

An emerging industrial cybersecurity startup speaking to potential customers and investors. The product is accurately described as a research prototype developed at FAST NUCES Islamabad. No production readiness, customers, certifications or performance statistics are invented.

## Approved direction

- Predominantly white, charcoal / navy typography, restrained lime and deep-teal actions.
- Circuit monogram recovered from the existing FYP poster's vector drawing; undistorted and reused across navigation, favicon, sharing preview and PDF.
- Centered headline: “See what’s happening in your industrial network.”
- Supporting line: “Offline-first OT monitoring. Local data. Explainable alerts.”
- Real dashboard evidence on a pale sage stage leads the homepage. Deep-teal editorial sections provide contrast.
- Home, Product, Research and About are separate pages. Architecture, protocol scope, intended applications and hardware are on Product.
- Explore the Platform is the main action. Download brief is secondary. Recording links become available only when the reviewed video exists.
- Fixed type sizes at explicit breakpoints, generous spacing, short readable paragraphs and restrained borders.
- Brief opacity/translation entrance animation and small hover transitions. Reduced motion removes both.
- Optional 3D is loaded only after a click, with a static illustration by default and a fallback when WebGL is unavailable. Rotation starts paused, is user controlled, and rendering stops while idle or offscreen.
- No invented portraits. Team names, proposed roles and public emails were explicitly approved in this conversation.
